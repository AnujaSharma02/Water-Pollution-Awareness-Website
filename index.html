What is Water Pollution?

Water pollution occurs when harmful substances contaminate water bodies. It affects aquatic life and human health.

Key Statistics

  • 80% of global wastewater is released untreated.
  • 1 in 3 people lack access to safe drinking water.

How You Can Help

  • Reduce plastic use
  • Dispose of chemicals properly
  • Join local clean-up events
